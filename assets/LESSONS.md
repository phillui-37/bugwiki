# Lessons

A rule arrives here on its **second occurrence**, not its first. The first
occurrence lives in `solutions/` only. The third promotes the rule into
`AGENTS.md` and the line leaves this file.

Vocabulary: `recurrence` counts occurrences on separate days. It is a number on
the page, not a judgement in your head.

```
promote to LESSONS.md (2 days): solutions/dsh/config-editor-rewrites-whole-document.md
```

## Why the gate

A `LESSONS.md` that accepts everything becomes a second changelog that nobody
reads. The bar is repetition: a rule earns a line when you had to relearn it, and
earns a place in `AGENTS.md` when it stops being about one tool.
